# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We expect everyone participating in VitaForge to be respectful, constructive,
and considerate. Welcome different perspectives, focus on the work, and avoid
personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, hateful conduct, deliberate
disruption, and publishing someone else's private information without consent.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **srikanthfernando3@gmail.com**. Include the
relevant discussion, repository location, and any supporting context. Reports
will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical.
