# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Vita Forge a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

- Be respectful in issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Give and accept constructive feedback
- Focus on what is best for the project

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, and publishing others’ private information.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **srikanthfernando3@gmail.com**. Maintainers will review reports and may remove content or ban contributors who violate this code.
